Our Story

Born from a passion for cycling — built for riders who refuse to settle.

Our Mission

"To connect every rider with the bicycle that changes their world."

We believe cycling is more than sport — it's freedom, community, and transformation. Since 2009, Velocity Cycles has been Colorado's premier destination for performance bicycles, guided by deep expertise and genuine passion for the ride.

Since 2009

15 Years of Riding Passion

It started in a small Denver garage. Co-founders Alex Park and Jordan Miles — both competitive cyclists — grew frustrated with the lack of expert-curated bicycle shops that truly understood the modern rider's needs.

In 2009, they launched Velocity Cycles with a simple commitment: stock only the world's finest bikes, employ only passionate riders, and deliver an experience that makes every customer feel like a pro.

Today, we serve over 50,000 cyclists across the United States, offering an expertly curated range of mountain, road, electric, and kids bicycles from the industry's most respected brands.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Passion First

Every team member is an active cyclist. We don't just sell bikes — we ride them, race them, and live them. This passion drives every recommendation we make.

Uncompromising Quality

We partner only with brands that share our commitment to excellence. Every bike in our range is chosen for its performance, reliability, and value.

Community First

Cycling builds community. We organize group rides, sponsor local races, and invest in trail development to grow the sport we love.
