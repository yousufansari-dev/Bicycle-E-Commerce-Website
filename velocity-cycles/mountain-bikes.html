Choosing Your Mountain Bike

Mountain bikes come in several disciplines, each optimized for specific riding styles. Understanding the differences helps you choose the perfect bike for your trails.

XC (Cross Country): Lightweight and efficient, perfect for racing and fitness riding on flowy trails.
Trail: The most versatile category — great climbers that can handle challenging descents.
Enduro: Built for aggressive descending with enough capability to pedal back up.
